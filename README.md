## 🤖 AI-Assisted Development

I see artificial intelligence as a tool to enhance a developer's productivity, not as a replacement for independent reasoning or technical judgment.

I use AI tools primarily to automate repetitive tasks, assist with documentation, explore alternative solutions, and streamline parts of the development workflow.

My approach is based on three principles:

- **🧠 Thinking remains the developer's responsibility.** Understanding the problem, designing the architecture, and making decisions about business logic require independent reasoning and technical judgment.
- **🔍 Code must be understood and verified.** Before incorporating a solution, I believe it is essential to understand what it does, why it works, and what potential consequences it may have.
- **⚙️ AI should improve productivity, not create dependency.** I use it to reduce mechanical work so I can dedicate more time to software design, problem-solving, and code quality.

I believe a developer's value goes beyond writing code. It comes from understanding problems, making sound technical decisions, and taking responsibility for the solutions they build.

**AI can help us work smarter, but technical judgment, understanding, and responsibility remain ours.**
